Quick breed guide

Dog Encyclopedia

Every dog is unique, but these notes are a helpful place to start.

Beagle

Size
Small to medium, with short legs and floppy ears.
Personality
Curious, cheerful, and always ready to follow a scent.
Everyday care
Needs daily walks, playtime, and regular ear checks.

Golden Retriever

Size
Medium to large, with a strong body and fluffy coat.
Personality
Friendly, gentle, and happy to learn new things.
Everyday care
Needs exercise, company, and frequent coat brushing.

Poodle

Size
Comes in toy, miniature, and standard sizes.
Personality
Smart, active, and often quick to learn tricks.
Everyday care
Needs play, training, and regular coat grooming.
